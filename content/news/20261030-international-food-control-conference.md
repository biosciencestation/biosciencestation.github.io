On 15–16 October 2026, BioScience Station members Nguyễn Dạ Hiền, Nguyễn Trà Giang and Ngô Quang Anh presented their research at the International Food Control Conference 2026 in Hanoi. Organised by the National Institute for Food Control, the conference brought researchers and food safety professionals together under the theme “Smart Food Safety Control toward International Harmonization”.

![BioScience Station members at IFCC 2026.](uploads/news/2026_IFCC%20%281%29.jpg)

At this year’s conference, Ngô Quang Anh received First Prize in the Young Scientist Session for his outstanding oral presentation of “Flavour-enhancing *Lactoplantibacillus plantarum* as biocontrol agents against molds from Dak Lak green coffee bean”. He isolated ten fungal strains and found that three bacterial strains and their cell-free supernatants inhibited mold growth in laboratory tests. One strain, BKHTA2, also showed antifungal activity on coffee beans.

![Ngô Quang Anh presenting his project in the Young Scientist Session.](uploads/news/2026_IFCC%20%284%29.jpg)

![Ngô Quang Anh receiving First Prize for his oral presentation in the Young Scientist Session.](uploads/news/2026_IFCC%20%283%29.jpg)

The study by Nguyễn Dạ Hiền and Nguyễn Trà Giang, “Potential application of bacteriophages for controlling multidrug-resistant *Enterococcus faecalis*: Toward microbial hazard control in food safety”, received the Third Prize Poster Presentation Award.

Congratulations to our members on successfully presenting their research findings! Hopefully, the conference gave them a wonderful opportunity to share their work and exchange valuable ideas with the wider food control research community.
