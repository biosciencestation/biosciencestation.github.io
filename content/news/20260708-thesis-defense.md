From July 7 to 10, 2026, the Department of Food Engineering and the Department of Bioengineering held their graduation project defense sessions. This year, five members of our lab presented their research before the academic committees. Their projects covered a wide range of topics, including the isolation of mold species, evaluation of antifungal activity, optimization of culture media for enzyme production, investigation of the synergistic effects of bacteriophages and antibiotics, and analysis of whole exome sequencing data.
