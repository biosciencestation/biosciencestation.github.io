Dr. Hòa is a lecturer at Hanoi University of Science and Technology, where she has taught and mentored students in microbiology and molecular biology for nearly two decades.

Her research sits at the intersection of biophotonics, microbiology, and biotechnology, with a current focus on coffee fermentation and cascara innovation: exploring how microbial processes shape flavor, aroma, and sustainability across the coffee value chain.

Dr. Hòa holds a PhD in Life Science and Technology from Tokyo Institute of Technology (Science Tokyo) and a master's degree in Biophotonics completed in Europe under an Erasmus Mundus scholarship. She has held visiting positions at BOKU Vienna, Osaka University, Science Tokyo, Ehime University, and Imperial College London.

Whether in the lab, the classroom, or on a coffee farm, she remains a committed learner, learning from colleagues, students, and the problems the team takes on together.
